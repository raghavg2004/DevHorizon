Strategic Advisory & Architecture Guidance to Accelerate Digital Growth

Navigating modern tech stacks, infrastructure investments, and software roadmaps can be daunting. DevHorizan delivers pragmatic, high-impact technical consulting tailored for early-stage founders, SMEs, and digital teams looking to make confident technology decisions.

Core Consultation Capabilities:
  • Tech Stack Selection & Evaluation – Advising on the most cost-effective and scalable programming languages, cloud frameworks, and databases for your product.
  • System Architecture Planning – Designing resilient microservices or modular monolith architectures built for high availability and low latency.

  • Agile Sprint & Delivery Strategy – Providing structured milestones, MVP scope prioritization, and accurate budget planning (₹3,500 – ₹25,000 range).
