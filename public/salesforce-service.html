Maximize Your CRM Potential with Certified Salesforce Engineering

Salesforce is the world's leading CRM platform powering enterprise growth. At DevHorizan, we deliver comprehensive Salesforce Administration and Custom Development services tailored to streamline operations, accelerate sales pipelines, and elevate customer engagement.

Key Aspects of Our Salesforce Services:
  • Salesforce Administration & Governance – Setting up Custom Objects, Fields, Page Layouts, Record Types, Profiles, Permission Sets, Role Hierarchies, and Validation Rules.
  • Flow Automation & Process Engineering – Designing Record-Triggered Flows, Screen Flows, Auto-launched Flows, and Approval Processes without technical debt.
  • Custom Apex Development – Crafting clean, scalable Apex Classes, Triggers, Batch Jobs, and Queueable jobs adhering strictly to governor limits and 90%+ unit test coverage.
  • Lightning Web Components (LWC) – Building modern, ultra-responsive, and reactive custom user interfaces for intuitive employee and partner experiences.
  • Third-Party API & ERP Integration – Integrating Salesforce with ERPs, payment gateways, mobile apps, and custom web portals using REST/SOAP APIs and Webhooks.
  • Sales Cloud & Service Cloud Optimization – Configuring lead scoring, opportunity pipelines, case management, omni-channel routing, and SLA milestones.
  • Data Migration & Security Audits – Performing large-scale data migrations via Data Loader and Bulk API, security health checks, and role-based access audits.
