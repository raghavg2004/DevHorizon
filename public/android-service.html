Mobile Engineering

High-Performance Native & Cross-Platform Mobile Solutions for iOS & Android

iOS and Android power over 99% of all mobile devices globally. At DevHorizan, we deliver bespoke, high-performance, and secure mobile applications engineered specifically for the Apple App Store and Google Play Store.

Key Aspects of Our iOS & Android Development Services:
  • Native iOS & Android Engineering – Building fluid, platform-native applications using Swift, SwiftUI, Kotlin, and Jetpack Compose to leverage device hardware and OS capabilities.
  • Cross-Platform Efficiency – Developing unified codebases with Flutter and React Native to accelerate time-to-market while maintaining a 60fps native feel.
  • MVVM & Clean Architecture – Ensuring enterprise-grade scalability, testability, offline caching, and modular maintainability.
  • Seamless Cloud, APIs & Payments – Real-time synchronization with RESTful / GraphQL endpoints, Firebase, Apple Pay, UPI direct integration, and push notification infrastructure.
  • Human Interface & Material Design UI – Crafting pixel-perfect, accessible interfaces compliant with Apple Human Interface Guidelines and Google Material Design 3.
  • App Store & Google Play Publishing – End-to-end release management, TestFlight beta distribution, security audits, App Store review compliance, and post-launch maintenance.
