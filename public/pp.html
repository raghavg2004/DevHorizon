Privacy Policy

Effective:

1. Introduction

At DevHorizan, we place the utmost value on client confidentiality and data security. This Privacy Policy details how we collect, process, and protect your information when you browse our website, request project quotes, or interact with our Client Portal.

2. Information We Collect

We only collect information required to evaluate, engineer, and deliver software solutions:

• Contact Information: Your name, email address, phone number, and company name provided via our contact forms or project estimator.

• Project Specifications: Feature checklists, technology stack preferences, and architectural requirements submitted through our estimator or client portal.

• Technical Log Data: Anonymous analytics including browser type, operating system, and session timestamps to continuously optimize platform performance.

3. How We Use Your Information

Your data is used solely to provide accurate cost estimations, build tailored software, manage project milestones, and communicate delivery status. DevHorizan does not sell, rent, monetize, or disclose your personal details or project concepts to any third party.

4. Enterprise-Grade Security

We deploy robust industry security practices including HTTPS/TLS 1.3 encryption in transit, bcrypt password hashing with salt rounds, parameterized database queries, and secure session management. Your intellectual property and repository tokens are stored in strictly isolated environments.

5. Payment Security & UPI Handling

DevHorizan supports zero-transaction-fee direct UPI verification and standard bank transfers. We never store debit/credit card numbers or UPI PINs on our servers. All transaction reference IDs are solely used for ledger reconciliation.

6. Your Rights & Data Ownership

You maintain full control of your personal data. At any time, you may request an export or complete deletion of your account, submitted requirements, or contact records by emailing our engineering leads at devhorizan@gmail.com.

7. Policy Revisions

We may periodically update this policy to reflect enhancements in our portal features or data protection regulations. The latest effective date will always be visible at the top of this document.
