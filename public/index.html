Frequently Asked Questions

What services does DevHorizan provide?

At DevHorizan, we offer a range of digital solutions, including iOS & Android Development, Web Development, Salesforce Admin & Development, UI/UX Design, Maintenance & Support, and Consultation. Our goal is to provide high-quality, tailored solutions to help businesses grow and succeed in the digital space.

How do I get started with a project?

Getting started is simple! You can contact us through our website, email, or phone. We will discuss your requirements, suggest the best approach, and provide a roadmap to execute your project efficiently.

Do you offer post-launch support and maintenance?

Absolutely! We provide ongoing maintenance and support to ensure your app or website remains updated, secure, and optimized for performance.

How long does a project take to complete?

Project timelines depend on the complexity and scope. A basic project may take a few weeks, while more advanced solutions can take several months. We ensure transparency and timely delivery.

Yes! We provide complete Salesforce administration and custom development, including Sales/Service Cloud setup, Flow automation, Apex coding, and Lightning Web Components (LWC) tailored to your business workflows.

Why should I choose DevHorizan?

At DevHorizan, we are committed to delivering high-quality solutions with a focus on innovation, user experience, and performance. Our team ensures seamless execution and long-term success for every project.
